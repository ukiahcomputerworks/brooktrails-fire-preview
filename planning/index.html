Planning & development

A clearer path from idea to approval.

Understand the Township and County review steps, gather the right documents, and contact staff before site work begins.

Know the handoff

District design review is not the County building permit.

Brooktrails reviews the project against district standards first. Mendocino County Planning & Building Services issues the construction permit. Some projects also need County transportation or septic approval.

Start here

Use the Brooktrails Design Review Application before submitting plans to the County. Confirm current requirements, fees, copies, and delivery instructions with district staff.

Your approval trail

Seven steps, in the order they matter.

Every project differs. This sequence shows who owns each decision and prevents a later-stage utility form from looking like the place to begin.

  1. 01
    Confirm the parcel and services

    Bring the street address and assessor’s parcel number. Ask the district to confirm water and sewer availability before investing in plans.

    Ask the District Desk →
  2. 02
    Prepare the district submission

    Gather the site plan, proposed work, grading and tree information, and the plan copies requested by the captured district standards.

    Read captured standards ↓
  3. 03
    Submit Brooktrails Design Review

    District staff and, when required, the District Architect review the project against the Brooktrails Specific Plan and district standards.

    Start with the application ↓
  4. 04
    Receive written district approval

    Keep the district approval with the plans you will submit to the County. Do not begin clearing, grading, or construction based on this preview.

  5. 05
    Apply for County permits

    Mendocino County issues building permits and provides the current application, digital-submittal process, fees, and plan-review requirements.

    Open County Planning & Building Services ↗
  6. 06
    Complete additional County reviews

    A driveway or work in the public right-of-way may require a Transportation encroachment permit. An unsewered property may require Environmental Health septic review.

    Transportation permits ↗Septic and land use ↗
  7. 07
    Coordinate construction utilities and final inspection

    After approvals, request the current construction-water or service application directly from the district. Confirm final district inspection requirements before occupancy or permanent service.

    Request current instructions →

Privacy correction

Do not email identity documents.

The captured legacy construction-meter form requested Social Security and driver’s-license numbers. It has been removed from this preview’s public files and search because ordinary email and a public website are not secure intake channels for that information.

Request the current form and approved delivery method directly from the district.

Long-range planning

Growth, water & a second access road.

The district describes its planning authority as a response to water-right and growth questions raised in the late 1980s. A second access road has remained an important planning element, with alternative-route work presented in 2009 and funding limitations reported in 2013.

Historical planning records remain available in the document center so current decisions can be read in context.
