Board of Directors

Five residents, one public trust.

The Board sets district policy and appoints the General Manager, General Counsel, and Auditor. Published source information lists Tina Tyler-O'Shea as President and Rick Williams as Vice-President, with Ed Horrick, Susan Mahoney, and Mary Ziady serving as directors.

Terms published by the district run through December 2026 or December 2028. Confirm current membership and meeting materials with the district office.
