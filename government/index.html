Civic file 03 · Follow through

Trace an issue from agenda to record.

Agendas show what was scheduled. Minutes and related records show what happened. The guided library keeps the captured meeting materials searchable.

  1. Start with the subject. Search a project, policy, or service.
  2. Compare agenda and minutes. Read the public record in sequence.
  3. Ask for the current record. Contact staff when the captured set is not enough.

Civic file 04 · Public money

Follow the plan, the rates, and the audit.

These records answer different questions: the financial plan sets direction, rates and fees affect service charges, and the annual audit reviews the completed fiscal year.

  1. Plan. Read the financial plan for services.
  2. Price. Review the adopted rates and fees.
  3. Verify. Read the annual audit.

Civic file 05 · Rules

Policy should be readable, not buried.

The policy manual guides Board and administrative practice. Ordinances, resolutions, and utility rules establish more specific requirements.

  1. Begin with the policy manual. Find the governing topic.
  2. Check the controlling record. Review relevant ordinances or resolutions.
  3. Confirm interpretation. Ask the district before acting on a legal or time-sensitive question.
