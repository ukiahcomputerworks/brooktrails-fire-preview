Brooktrails Redwood Park

Conservation land woven through the community.

Approximately 2,500 acres were dedicated by the original developer for conservation and recreation. The district continued that intent through Brooktrails Redwood Park, including active-use, neighborhood-use, and passive-use areas.

Ordinance 63 formally established the park in 1988. Ordinance 68 adopted a master plan later that year. A Greenbelt Stewardship Plan was adopted by Resolution 2013-14 after years of community committee work.
