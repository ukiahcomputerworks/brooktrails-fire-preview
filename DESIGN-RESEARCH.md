# Township and special-district comparison

Reviewed September 10, 2026.

## Sites reviewed

- Nipomo Community Services District, <https://ncsd.ca.gov/>: prominent resident quick links for billing, conservation, forms, FAQs, office contact, and board information.
- Granada Community Services District, <https://granada.ca.gov/>: featured service cards, meeting calendar, sewer emergency access, and issue reporting.
- Wrightwood Community Services District, <https://www.wrightwoodcsd.org/>: immediate next-meeting visibility, short quick-link set, and announcements.
- Cosumnes Community Services District, <https://cosumnescsd.gov/>: clear district purpose and distinct Fire, Parks and Recreation, and Planning and Design service areas.
- Hidden Valley Lake Community Services District, <https://www.hvlcsd.org/>: service interruption notices, board materials, utility billing, and alerts.
- Loleta Community Services District, <https://www.loletacommunityservicesdistrict.gov/board-meetings>: year-grouped agendas and minutes.
- Templeton Community Services District, <https://templetoncsd.org/AgendaCenter>: searchable, categorized meeting records.

## Applied findings

1. Put high-frequency resident actions directly below the opening message.
2. Organize navigation around services and tasks, not the old site's internal page structure.
3. Make emergency actions visually unmistakable and link changing incident information to responsible agencies.
4. Give board materials and public records a dedicated, searchable destination.
5. Surface contact information in the header, footer, and a cross-agency directory.
6. Preserve the district's local character with real Brooktrails imagery and landscape-derived colors.
7. Keep meeting and alert infrastructure future-ready without fabricating live data in a concept preview.

No comparison-site copy or design was duplicated. The preview combines common civic usability patterns with Brooktrails source content and an original presentation.
