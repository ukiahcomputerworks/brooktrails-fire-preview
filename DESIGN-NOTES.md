# Design and content decisions

The redesign treats Brooktrails as one public-service district, while preserving a strong, distinct Fire and Emergency Safety destination. Resident tasks appear before organization structure. Emergency action stays visible without turning every district page into a fire website.

The visual system draws from the local landscape: deep redwood green for civic framing, cream and paper surfaces for legibility, gold for wayfinding, and ember red reserved for urgent fire content. Editorial serif headlines add a sense of place; plain system sans serif text keeps operational information easy to scan.

Changing facts are framed carefully. Meeting materials, connection availability, employment openings, burn status, and office-holder information direct readers to confirm with the district or responsible public agency. Emergency actions call 911 directly. The preview does not simulate live alerts, payment processing, or official forms.

All locally retained source images and documents are used only for this `noindex` redesign preview. The station exterior came from a public map listing and must be replaced with a department-owned original before production. Third-party historical reproductions should receive a rights review before any production launch.
