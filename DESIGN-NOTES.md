# Design and content decisions

The redesign treats Brooktrails as one public-service district, while preserving a strong, distinct Fire and Emergency Safety destination. Resident tasks appear before organization structure. Emergency action stays visible without turning every district page into a fire website.

The September 29 revision makes an experiential leap under the name **Redwood Afterglow**. It uses the emotional logic of a rewarding, destination-quality experience without copying the Alpha After Dark look. The visual language belongs to Brooktrails: nocturnal redwood greens, creek-light cyan, lantern gold, warm mist text, topographic linework, and ember red reserved for urgent fire content. Architectural sans serif headlines create a distinctive trail-marker voice, with selective serif phrasework adding a softer sense of place; plain system sans serif text keeps operational information easy to scan.

The home page is now a field guide rather than an agency landing page. “Choose your trail” frames six service destinations as tactile, numbered markers. Landscape photography is atmospheric rather than decorative, while metrics and calls to action provide small moments of discovery and confidence. Interior routes stay calmer and more operational so the visual reward never competes with records, instructions, or emergency information.

Motion is progressive enhancement. Elements reveal only when JavaScript, viewport observation, and the visitor's motion preference allow it. Pointer lighting is decorative and does not carry meaning. Keyboard focus, touch targets, reading order, and print output remain explicit parts of the system.

Changing facts are framed carefully. Meeting materials, connection availability, employment openings, burn status, and office-holder information direct readers to confirm with the district or responsible public agency. Emergency actions call 911 directly. The preview does not simulate live alerts, payment processing, or official forms.

All locally retained source images and documents are used only for this `noindex` redesign preview. The station exterior came from a public map listing and must be replaced with a department-owned original before production. Third-party historical reproductions should receive a rights review before any production launch. Production `btcsd.org`, forms, payments, emergency systems, and DNS remain outside this preview's authority.
