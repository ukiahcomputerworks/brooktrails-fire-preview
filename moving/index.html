Before you buy or move

Know the forest—and the systems beneath it.

Brooktrails is a distinctive community, not a city with every service under one roof. This orientation separates what the district provides from questions owned by Mendocino County and other organizations.

Bring one useful key

A street address or assessor’s parcel number helps staff answer property-specific questions about district service and planning review.
