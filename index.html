Built around this place

A forest community with public service at its core.

Brooktrails sits northwest of Willits in northern Mendocino County. The district helps protect essential infrastructure, public safety, local recreation, and roughly 2,500 acres dedicated as conservation land in Brooktrails Redwood Park.
