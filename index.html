Ready before the smoke

Prepare your home and household.

Give residents one reliable place to find practical actions, with official resources linked at the moment they are needed.

Evacuation

Know what you will take and where you will go.

Plan two exit routes, prepare a go bag, account for pets and mobility needs, and keep vehicles ready during elevated fire conditions.

Explore Ready for Wildfire evacuation guidance ↗

Defensible space

Create room for firefighters to work.

Start closest to the structure. Clear combustible debris, maintain vegetation, and review current California guidance before seasonal work.

Review defensible space guidance ↗

Burn permits

Check both fire and air quality requirements.

As of May 2024, permits are issued by CAL FIRE and the Mendocino County Air Quality Management District. Brooktrails does not determine burn season or perform smoke checks.

A simpler resident experience

Start routine requests online.

These examples show how secure Microsoft 365 workflows could route information to department staff. They are demonstrations only and do not send, store, or submit data.

Concept workflow: A live version could validate a request, create a tracked Microsoft 365 record, notify the correct team, and return a reference number to the resident. Department review and configuration would be required before activation.
