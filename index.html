Burn day and permit center

Check two authorities before you burn.

CAL FIRE controls seasonal fire-safety status. Mendocino County Air Quality Management District makes the daily permissive burn-day determination. Approval from one does not replace the other.

CAL FIRE status for Mendocino County

Burning suspended

Effective June 15, 2026 at 8:00 AM for all State Responsibility Area land in Mendocino County.

Verified from CAL FIRE on August 27, 2026. Conditions can change.

Annual calendar

Automatic no-burn dates

These Mendocino AQMD dates recur every year. Seasonal suspension, weather, or a daily no-burn determination can add more restricted days.

  • Jan1New Year's Day
  • May25Memorial Day 2026
  • Jul4Independence Day
  • Oct31Halloween
  • Nov26-27Thanksgiving and following day 2026
  • Dec25Christmas Day

Calendar dates are a planning aid, not a burn authorization. Always verify CAL FIRE status and call the daily AQMD line.

Ready before the smoke

Prepare your home and household.

Regional fire-department sites consistently prioritize evacuation zones, alert enrollment, defensible space, and clear official links. This concept puts those actions first.

Evacuation

Know what you will take and where you will go.

Plan two exit routes, prepare a go bag, account for pets and mobility needs, and keep vehicles ready during elevated fire conditions.

Explore Ready for Wildfire evacuation guidance ↗

Defensible space

Create room for firefighters to work.

Start closest to the structure. Clear combustible debris, maintain vegetation, and review current California guidance before seasonal work.
