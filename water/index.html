Brooktrails water system

Surface water, stored close to home.

The district describes two reservoirs totaling about 400 acre-feet on Willits Creek and a tributary. Lake Emily feeds Lake Ada Rose before water moves to the treatment plant.

The public system description includes a 1.2 million gallon-per-day treatment plant, 60 miles of mains, 24 water tanks, and 18 pump stations. Annual Consumer Confidence Reports provide testing and water-quality results.

Conservation

Save water when the power goes out.

The system relies on electric pumps to fill storage tanks. During outages, using less water helps the community maintain service while generators support the system.

Every water customer is subject to a 9,000 gallon monthly use limit. The district's conservation resources cover meter reading, indoor and outdoor efficiency, rainwater, graywater, pools, irrigation, and professional resources.

Wastewater

Sewer service & lateral inspections

The district provides wastewater collection for most of Brooktrails, while Spring Creek, Sylvandale, and some higher-elevation Brooktrails properties are not served by the collection system. Wastewater is treated at the City of Willits Wastewater Treatment Plant.

Sewer lateral inspections are required when an owner or tenant moves into a residence that has not been inspected within the last five years. Check with the district office for the address's last inspection date.
