Mendocino County Air Quality
03

Air-quality burn permit

Apply online with the Air Quality Management District or use its current mail-in PDF. This is separate from the CAL FIRE permit.

Fill out online ↗Download mail-in form ↗

Call the burn-day line before ignition: 707-463-4391.

Before you burn

Permit is not permission.

Residents may need both CAL FIRE and Air Quality approval. Even with permits in hand, burning is allowed only when restrictions are lifted and the day is declared permissive.

  1. Check CAL FIRE's current Mendocino County status.
  2. Confirm the Air Quality burn-day determination.
  3. Follow every condition printed on both permits.

Brooktrails does not set burn season

The district's current source directs permit questions to CAL FIRE and Mendocino County Air Quality. For neighborhood smoke questions, call CAL FIRE Willits at 707-459-7414.
